# Higgsfield Marketing Studio — Founder Direct-to-Camera (30s)

**Brand:** Educare Leads · **Offer:** Done-for-you enrollment system → full calendar in 90 days
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 30 seconds · 6 clips (~5s each) · paid social (Meta/TikTok/YT Shorts)

---

## 1) GLOBAL STYLE (set once in Higgsfield, applied to every clip)

> **Subject / presenter:** A warm, credible business owner, 32–45, business-casual (navy blazer or smart knit, no tie). Friendly, confident, direct eye contact. *Lock a single reference image / character so the face stays consistent across all 6 clips.*
>
> **Setting:** Bright, modern childcare / school front office — soft natural window light, tidy shelves, a few tasteful kid-friendly details (cubbies, plants, a wall of framed art) blurred in the background. Premium, private-school feel — clean, not cluttered.
>
> **Lighting / grade:** Soft key light, shallow depth of field (background bokeh). Color grade leaning into the brand palette: deep navy shadows, clean whites, subtle cyan/teal accents.
>
> **Lens / framing:** 35–50mm look, medium close-up (chest up), eyes on upper third.
>
> **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Motion:** subtle, realistic — no warping faces.
>
> **Voice (if using AI VO):** warm, measured, trustworthy American adult; conversational pace, light authority. NOT hypey/announcer.
>
> **Caption style:** bold condensed sans (Archivo Black look), white with a cyan (#11A0D8) highlight on the key word; gold (#F5C518) only for money figures. Lower-third, large.

---

## 2) CLIP-BY-CLIP SCRIPT

Paste each **Visual prompt** into a clip, set the **Camera move**, drop the **Voiceover** into the VO/script field, and add the **Caption** as on-screen text.

### Clip 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:05)
- **Visual prompt:** Business owner stands in a bright daycare front office, looks straight into the lens, calm and direct. Soft window light, shallow depth of field.
- **Camera move:** Slow push-in (dolly in), subtle.
- **Voiceover:** "Every empty spot in your program is quietly costing you a thousand to two thousand dollars a month."
- **Caption:** EVERY EMPTY SPOT = **$1–2K/MONTH**

### Clip 2 — AGITATE (0:05–0:10)
- **Visual prompt:** Same owner, slight head tilt, empathetic expression; behind them a phone sits unanswered on the desk, a calendar with open slots faintly visible.
- **Camera move:** Static, locked-off.
- **Voiceover:** "And most owners are stuck chasing leads while families quietly slip through the cracks."
- **Caption:** CHASING LEADS ALL DAY?

### Clip 3 — THE SYSTEM (0:10–0:17)
- **Visual prompt:** Owner gestures confidently; quick tasteful cutaway inserts (1s each) of: a phone screen showing an instant text reply, a Meta/Google ad, a calendar filling with booked slots. Return to owner.
- **Camera move:** Owner shot static; inserts are quick punch-in cuts.
- **Voiceover:** "So we install a complete done-for-you system — local ads, an A.I. assistant that books families in seconds, and a CRM that tracks every lead."
- **Caption:** WE INSTALL THE WHOLE **SYSTEM**

### Clip 4 — OUTCOME + TIMEFRAME (0:17–0:23)
- **Visual prompt:** Owner smiles slightly, relaxed and assured; soft rack-focus from background to their face. Warm, optimistic tone.
- **Camera move:** Gentle push-in.
- **Voiceover:** "We run all of it for you — and fill your calendar with qualified families within ninety days."
- **Caption:** A FULL CALENDAR IN **90 DAYS**

### Clip 5 — TRUST / SPECIALIZATION (0:23–0:27)
- **Visual prompt:** Owner, arms relaxed, nods once; behind them a warm, full classroom scene (children with a teacher) softly out of focus.
- **Camera move:** Static.
- **Voiceover:** "Childcare and education programs are all we do — daycares, schools, and camps."
- **Caption:** CHILDCARE & EDUCATION **ONLY**

### Clip 6 — CTA (0:27–0:30)
- **Visual prompt:** Owner steps slightly toward camera, points/gestures down toward the CTA; clean navy end-frame begins to wipe in with logo space.
- **Camera move:** Subtle push-in, then hold on end card.
- **Voiceover:** "Book a free call — the link's right below."
- **Caption:** 👇 BOOK YOUR FREE CALL · **Educare Leads**
