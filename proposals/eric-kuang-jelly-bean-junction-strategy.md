# 6-Month Enrollment Recovery Strategy

**Prepared for:** Eric Kuang - Jelly Bean Junction Early Learning Center  
**Location:** Melbourne, FL  
**Prepared by:** EduCare Leads (9503-3692 Quebec Inc.)  
**Date:** August 18, 2026

---

## 1. Core Goal

Jelly Bean Junction currently has **112 enrolled children** against an actual operating capacity of **190**.

The practical target is to reach **150 enrolled children by February 2027**, which requires **38 net new enrollments** over roughly 6 months. The longer-term target is to keep moving toward the full **190-child actual operating capacity**.

To reach 150, the center needs to average roughly:

- **6-7 net new enrollments per month**
- **1-2 net new enrollments per week**
- Enough qualified tour volume to account for no-shows, unresponsive parents, and families who are not the right fit

Because Eric has been burned by low-priority leads before, this strategy prioritizes **lead quality, verification, and conversion tracking** over raw lead volume.

---

## 2. Positioning Strategy

The core message should be:

**A large, well-resourced early learning center that still feels personal, well-staffed, and family-centered.**

Key proof points to build around:

- Licensed for 330 with actual operating capacity of 190
- Programs from infants at 6 weeks through 6 years old
- Before- and after-school programs
- Successful summer camp with **145 children**
- Well-staffed classrooms
- Quality furniture and center environment
- A leadership philosophy built around happiness for families, staff, and children

Eric's “run it like Costco” idea is useful. It means the marketing should communicate trust, consistency, value, care, and a happy experience for everyone involved.

---

## 3. Phase 1 - Tracking & Lead Quality Foundation

**Timeline:** Week 1-2

The first priority is to make the enrollment system measurable.

Actions:

- Define what counts as a qualified lead.
- Create CRM stages: new lead, qualified, contacted, tour booked, tour showed, enrolled, lost / not fit.
- Track source by channel: word of mouth, Google Ads, Meta ads, referral, website, phone, walk-in.
- Add classroom and age-group tagging.
- Add parent start timeline: now, 30 days, 60 days, later.
- Review current Google Ads structure, search terms, landing page, and lead quality.
- Create simple weekly reporting around leads, tours, enrollments, and conversion rates.

Goal:

Eric should no longer have to guess whether marketing is working. Every lead should have a source, status, next step, and quality signal.

---

## 4. Phase 2 - Trust Assets & Proof

**Timeline:** Week 1-4

Eric said client verification, testimonials, case studies, and a clear gameplan would make him more confident. Those same assets will also make parents more confident.

Actions:

- Collect 10-15 written parent testimonials.
- Collect 2-3 short video testimonials if possible.
- Create a “why families choose Jelly Bean Junction” proof section.
- Highlight the successful summer camp with 145 children.
- Capture photos or short clips of classrooms, furniture, staff warmth, and center quality.
- Build proof into ad creative, landing pages, follow-up texts, and tour reminders.
- Prepare a simple monthly performance case-study format showing leads, tours, enrollments, and lessons learned.

Goal:

Parents should quickly see that Jelly Bean Junction is real, trusted, established, and worth touring.

---

## 5. Phase 3 - Campaign Launch

**Timeline:** Weeks 2-6

The campaign should start with controlled testing, not broad random lead volume.

Campaign angles:

- Infant care starting at 6 weeks
- Early learning for toddlers and preschoolers
- Before- and after-school care
- “Large center resources, smaller classroom feel”
- Summer camp success and year-round family trust
- Well-staffed, warm, high-quality environment

Lead form filters:

- Child age
- Desired start date
- Care schedule needed
- Program interest
- Parent phone number and email
- Best time to contact
- Zip code or neighborhood

Follow-up flow:

- Instant SMS confirming inquiry
- Email with program highlights and proof
- Call task for staff or EduCare team
- Tour booking link or direct scheduling prompt
- Reminder before tour
- Post-tour follow-up

Goal:

Increase the number of qualified inquiries while reducing the amount of staff time wasted on low-intent leads.

---

## 6. Phase 4 - Conversion Optimization

**Timeline:** Month 2-4

Once leads are flowing, the focus shifts to improving conversion.

Actions:

- Review lead-to-tour conversion rate.
- Review tour show rate.
- Review tour-to-enrollment conversion rate.
- Identify where families drop off.
- Improve call scripts, SMS scripts, and email follow-up.
- Adjust landing page copy based on parent questions and objections.
- Test testimonials and proof assets in ads and follow-up.
- Segment follow-up by infant care, preschool, and before/after school.

Goal:

Turn more of the existing interest into tours and more tours into enrollments.

---

## 7. Phase 5 - Scale Toward 150

**Timeline:** Month 4-6

Once the system shows which leads and age groups convert best, spend and creative should be focused on the highest-value opportunities.

Actions:

- Prioritize rooms with the most immediate enrollment capacity.
- Increase ad spend only when lead quality and conversion are clear.
- Run retargeting to parents who clicked, visited, or started forms but did not book.
- Promote limited openings by age group.
- Add referral pushes to current families.
- Use summer camp proof to support year-round enrollment.
- Continue weekly pipeline review.

Goal:

Reach **140-150 enrolled children by the start of 2027** and at least **150 enrolled by February 2027**.

## 8. Weekly Scorecard

Each week, Eric should be able to see:

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| New leads | Measures demand created |
| Qualified leads | Protects staff time |
| Booked tours | Shows real parent intent |
| Tour show rate | Shows follow-up quality |

| Lead source | Shows which channels work |
| Age-group demand | Shows which rooms to prioritize |
| Cost per qualified lead | Shows ad efficiency |
| Cost per booked tour | Shows pipeline efficiency |
