1. The Opportunity

Every empty seat in your Montessori program is recurring tuition that never arrives. Most schools rely on word-of-mouth, the occasional Google search, and open houses that fill slowly — leaving classrooms below capacity and growth on the table.

EduCare Leads installs a complete, done-for-you enrollment system that puts qualified local families in front of your program and books them onto tours — so your admissions calendar stays full.

2. What We Install — The EduCare Ecosystem

  • Website / landing page — a conversion-focused page built to turn local parents into booked tours.
  • Ad creatives & campaigns — hyper-local Meta ads, written and designed for your Montessori program and the families nearby.
  • Automations & AI follow-up — every inquiry is texted, qualified, and booked in seconds, so no lead goes cold.
  • Channels & tracking — all leads captured and tracked in your CRM, from first click to enrolled family.
