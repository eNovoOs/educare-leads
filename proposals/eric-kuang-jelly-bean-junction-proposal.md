# Enrollment Recovery Proposal

**Prepared for:** Eric Kuang - Jelly Bean Junction Early Learning Center  
**Location:** Melbourne, FL  
**Program type:** Large childcare center  
**Prepared by:** EduCare Leads (9503-3692 Quebec Inc.)  
**Date:** August 18, 2026

---

## 1. The Opportunity

Jelly Bean Junction Early Learning Center is a large childcare center in Melbourne, FL with programs serving children from **6 weeks old through 6 years old**, plus before- and after-school care.

The center has:

- **330 licensed capacity**
- **190 actual operating capacity** based on staffing, cubbies, and room readiness
- **112 seats currently filled**
- **78 open seats**

Eric bought the school 5 years ago when enrollment was around **154 children**. The last couple of years have been on the decline, and the center is now at 112 enrolled. Eric's expectation is to reach at least **150 enrolled children by February 2027** and begin next year around **140-150 enrolled**.

That means the immediate enrollment recovery target is **38 additional enrollments** to reach 150, with a longer-term opportunity to fill the remaining actual operating capacity.

---

## 2. Revenue Gap

Using the center's average tuition of **$260 per week**, the 78 open seats represent:

- **$20,280 per week** in missed tuition
- **$81,120 per 4-week month** in missed tuition
- **$973,440 per year** in missed tuition

Infants are especially valuable, with tuition at **$300 per week**. Because every room is approximately **2/3 full**, the opportunity is not limited to one classroom. The growth plan needs to improve inquiry quality and conversion across the whole center.

The center currently produces about **$100,000 per month** in revenue. Recovering enrollment toward 150 children would help Eric regain momentum, stabilize operations, and reduce the pressure of having to wear every hat himself.

---

## 3. Current Marketing Reality

Jelly Bean Junction has strong assets, but the current enrollment engine is not predictable enough:

- Word of mouth is currently the #1 source of inquiries.
- Google Ads started last week, but budget and conversion performance are not yet clear.
- The center averages about **5 new leads per week**.
- Conversion rate is not currently tracked.
- A previous service produced low-priority leads, forcing Eric and staff to spend time calling unqualified families.

Because of that experience, this plan needs to focus on **qualified leads**, not just more leads. The goal is to protect Eric and his staff from wasted follow-up while building confidence through tracking, proof, and a clear plan.

---

## 4. Recommended Plan: Enrollment Growth Partnership

EduCare Leads will build and manage an enrollment system designed to help Jelly Bean Junction move from **112 enrolled children toward 150 by February 2027**.

### What we manage

- **Local Meta ads** - Facebook and Instagram campaigns targeting families near Jelly Bean Junction
- **Google Ads review** - review current search traffic, landing experience, and lead quality
- **Lead qualification** - forms and filters that identify age group, start timeline, care needs, and parent fit
- **CRM setup and management** - every lead organized by classroom, program, source, and enrollment stage
- **Follow-up automations** - text and email follow-up so interested families receive fast replies
- **Tour booking support** - helping move qualified families from inquiry to scheduled tour
- **Full CRO review** - a conversion review of the enrollment path, messaging, lead forms, landing pages, mobile experience, and parent inquiry flow
- **Proof and trust assets** - support collecting and using testimonials, parent verification, case-study style proof, and center differentiators
- **Reporting** - visibility into ad spend, lead quality, booked tours, conversion rate, and enrollment progress

## 6. Why This Approach Fits Jelly Bean Junction

Eric does not need random lead volume. He needs a system that brings in parents who are real, qualified, and ready to consider enrollment.

The center already has strong positioning assets:

- A large center with real operating capacity
- Programs from infants through 6 years old
- Before- and after-school care
- A successful summer camp that served **145 children**
- A focus on being well staffed
- Quality furniture and a well-maintained center environment
- A clear operating philosophy: run the center like Costco, where families, staff, and children are happy

The enrollment system should turn those strengths into local trust, better inquiries, booked tours, and a measurable path back to 150 enrolled children.

---

## 7. What Success Looks Like

Success will be measured by:

- Qualified local family inquiries
- Lead quality by classroom and program
- Booked tours
- Tour show rate
- Lead-to-tour conversion rate
- Tour-to-enrollment conversion rate
- New enrollments generated
- Progress from 112 to 150 enrolled children by February 2027
- Longer-term progress toward 190 actual operating capacity
