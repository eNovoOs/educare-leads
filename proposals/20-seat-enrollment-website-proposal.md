## 1. The Opportunity

You currently have **20 open seats** that need to be filled as soon as possible.

At **$800 per month per enrollment**, those seats represent:

- **$16,000 per month** in potential recurring tuition once filled
- **$192,000 per year** in potential tuition
- Up to **$24,000 per child** over a 30-month enrollment window from age 2.5 to 5

Because families can remain enrolled for multiple years, each new enrollment is not just a one-time sale. It is a long-term revenue opportunity for the center.

Right now, your local Facebook activity is already generating interest - approximately **5 interested families per week**. The goal is to turn that interest into a more consistent, trackable enrollment system that brings in qualified families, follows up quickly, books tours, and helps fill those seats.
