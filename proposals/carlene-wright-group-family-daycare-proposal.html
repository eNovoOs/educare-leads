2. Recommended Plan: Enrollment Growth Partnership

Recommended path: Meta ads + CRM + follow-up + website

With 9 open seats, the goal is to turn local parent attention into a consistent flow of inquiries, booked tours, and enrollments.

  • Local Meta ads - Facebook and Instagram campaigns targeted to families near the daycare.
  • Campaign strategy - positioning, messaging, creative direction, audience testing, and optimization.
  • Lead capture - forms and landing pages designed to turn parent interest into inquiries.
  • CRM setup and management - every lead organized in one simple system.
  • Follow-up automations - text and email follow-up so interested families do not fall through the cracks.
  • Tour booking support - helping move families from inquiry to scheduled tour.
  • Website build and hosting - a professional website that presents the program clearly and converts local parents.
  • Reporting - clear visibility into ad spend, leads, booked tours, and enrollment progress.

4. Why the Partnership Option Makes Sense

The website matters, but with 9 open seats to fill, the bigger opportunity is the full enrollment system:

  • Ads generate local parent attention.
  • The website and landing pages turn that attention into inquiries.
  • CRM and automations make sure every lead is followed up with quickly.
  • Tour booking creates the path from interest to enrollment.
  • Ongoing management keeps the campaign improving instead of sitting still.

With each enrollment worth $360 per week, even a small number of new enrollments can quickly offset the monthly marketing investment.

5. What Success Looks Like

The immediate goal is to build a consistent pipeline of local families and begin filling the 9 available seats as quickly as possible.

  • Enrollments generated
  • Progress toward filling all 9 open seats
