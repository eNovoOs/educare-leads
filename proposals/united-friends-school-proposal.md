## 1. A True Partnership

United Friends School has spent more than 40 years building something rare: a warm, values-driven Quaker community that families love. Our role isn't to change who you are - it's to make sure every family who *would* love UFS actually finds you, understands what makes you different, and enrolls.

We don't operate like an outside agency. **We embed as your internal enrollment and growth team** - an extension of your staff that owns the marketing, technology, and enrollment engine so your team can stay focused on educating children.

---

## 2. What We Manage - The Full EduCare Ecosystem

As your partner, we take ownership of the systems that drive enrollment and growth:

- **Enrollment management** - we run the entire family journey: inquiry, nurture, tour booking, follow-up, and conversion, so no prospective family slips through the cracks.
- **Website management** - your site is hosted, maintained, updated, and continuously improved by us. New pages, new programs, seasonal updates - handled.
- **Paid advertising** - we run and manage **Meta ads (Facebook & Instagram)** and **Google ads**, with ongoing creative and landing pages that tell the UFS story authentically and bring qualified local families to your door.
- **AI-powered front desk** - **AI chatbots** and **AI receptionists** answer families instantly, day or night; **automated email & SMS feedback loops** nurture every inquiry; and **tour bookings** are scheduled automatically onto your calendar.
- **Positioning & offer strategy** - we help you stand out in your market: how to present your programs, your value, and your enrollment offers.
- **Growth & program innovation** - we work alongside leadership to identify opportunities to grow and evolve your programs (new offerings, summer camp, after-school, community classes, and beyond).
- **Automations & CRM** - every lead, family, and follow-up tracked and automated inside the EduCare platform.

---

## 3. How We Work Together

- **Embedded team.** Think of us as in-house, not outsourced - accessible, proactive, and invested in UFS.
- **Regular collaboration.** Ongoing strategy and working sessions with your leadership to align on goals, programs, and priorities.
- **Full transparency.** Clear reporting on enrollment activity, marketing performance, and what's working.
- **We define success together.** From day one we agree on what winning looks like for UFS - and build toward it.
