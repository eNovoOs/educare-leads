1. The Opportunity

Moira operates two childcare centers: Chestnut Treehouse in Lithonia, GA and Chestnut Treehouse 3 in Lawrenceville, GA, approximately 30 minutes apart.

Location #1 is licensed for 148 children and is currently sitting at 46 enrolled, leaving approximately 102 open seats. Location #2 has a similar opportunity, but the full classroom breakdown was not available at the time of this proposal.

Practical enrollment target: Moira said that reaching 85 children at each center would make operations and day-to-day life easier. That creates a clear target of 170 total children across both locations. For Location #1, that means moving from 46 to 85 children - 39 additional enrollments.

Moira is competing in a dense market, with approximately 52 childcare competitors within a 20-mile radius, including nearby competitors such as Creekstone and Childtime. Chestnut Treehouse is not a small center. The key positioning opportunity is to communicate that families get the strength and stability of a larger licensed center while still benefiting from the care, attention, and comfort of smaller class sizes.

Right now, enrollment marketing is mostly offline: banners in front of the locations and flyers handed out to local businesses. There are currently no paid ads running, which means Moira is competing in a crowded market without a consistent digital enrollment engine.

3. Recommended Plan: Two-Location Enrollment Growth Partnership

Recommended path: Meta ads + CRM + CRO + follow-up

Moira needs a two-location digital enrollment engine that turns local parent attention into inquiries, booked tours, and enrollments - with each center moving toward the 85-child operating target.

  • Local Meta ads - Facebook and Instagram campaigns targeting families near Lithonia and Lawrenceville.
  • Two-location campaign structure - location-specific targeting, creative, and lead tracking.
  • Competitive positioning - messaging that highlights smaller class sizes, trust, stability, and convenience.
  • Lead capture - forms and landing pages designed to turn parent interest into inquiries.
  • CRM setup and management - every lead organized by location, age group, and enrollment stage.
  • Follow-up automations - text and email follow-up so interested families do not fall through the cracks.
  • Tour booking support - helping move families from inquiry to scheduled tour.
  • Full CRO review - a conversion review of the enrollment path, messaging, lead forms, landing pages, and parent inquiry flow.
  • Reporting - visibility into ad spend, leads, booked tours, age-group demand, and enrollment progress.

5. Why the Partnership Option Makes Sense

Conversion matters, but with two locations, 52 nearby competitors, and a major capacity gap, the bigger opportunity is the full enrollment system:

  • Ads generate consistent local parent attention.
  • Landing pages, forms, and inquiry flow turn that attention into inquiries.
  • CRM and automations make sure every lead is followed up with quickly.
  • Tour booking creates the path from interest to enrollment.
  • Reporting shows which age groups and locations are converting best.
  • Ongoing management keeps campaigns improving instead of sitting still.

With Location #1 alone representing approximately $48,360 per month in paid infant through 3-year-old open-seat opportunity, even a small number of new enrollments can quickly offset the monthly marketing investment.

6. What Success Looks Like

The immediate goal is to build a consistent pipeline of local families for both Chestnut Treehouse locations, beginning with the largest revenue gaps at Location #1 and moving toward 85 children enrolled at each center.

  • Qualified local family inquiries
  • Booked tours by location
  • Leads by age group
  • Follow-up response rates
  • Cost per lead and cost per booked tour
  • Enrollments generated
  • Progress toward 85 children enrolled at each center
  • Location #1 progress from 46 to 85 children
  • Progress toward filling priority classrooms and afterschool openings
