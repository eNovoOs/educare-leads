1. The Opportunity

Jelly Bean Junction Early Learning Center is a large childcare center in Melbourne, FL with programs serving children from 6 weeks old through 6 years old, plus before- and after-school care.

190
Actual operating capacity based on staff, cubbies, and room readiness
112

78

Eric bought the school 5 years ago when enrollment was around 154 children. The last couple of years have been on the decline, and the center is now at 112 enrolled.

Practical enrollment target: Eric expects at least 150 enrolled children by February 2027 and wants to start next year around 140-150 enrolled. That means the immediate recovery target is 38 additional enrollments.

2. Revenue Gap

Using the center's average tuition of $260 per week, the 78 open seats represent:

Infants are especially valuable, with tuition at $300 per week. Because every room is approximately 2/3 full, the growth plan needs to improve inquiry quality and conversion across the whole center.

The center currently produces about $100,000 per month in revenue. Recovering enrollment toward 150 children would help Eric regain momentum, stabilize operations, and reduce the pressure of having to wear every hat himself.

3. Current Marketing Reality

  • Word of mouth is currently the #1 source of inquiries.
  • Google Ads started last week, but budget and conversion performance are not yet clear.
  • The center averages about 5 new leads per week.
  • Conversion rate is not currently tracked.
  • A previous service produced low-priority leads, forcing Eric and staff to spend time calling unqualified families.

Because of that experience, this plan needs to focus on qualified leads, not just more leads. The goal is to protect Eric and his staff from wasted follow-up while building confidence through tracking, proof, and a clear plan.

4. Recommended Plan: Enrollment Growth Partnership

Recommended path: qualified leads + CRM + CRO + proof

EduCare Leads will build and manage an enrollment system designed to help Jelly Bean Junction move from 112 enrolled children toward 150 by February 2027.

  • Local Meta ads - Facebook and Instagram campaigns targeting families near Jelly Bean Junction.
  • Google Ads review - review current search traffic, landing experience, and lead quality.
  • Lead qualification - forms and filters that identify age group, start timeline, care needs, and parent fit.
  • CRM setup and management - every lead organized by classroom, program, source, and enrollment stage.
  • Follow-up automations - text and email follow-up so interested families receive fast replies.
  • Tour booking support - helping move qualified families from inquiry to scheduled tour.
  • Full CRO review - a conversion review of the enrollment path, messaging, lead forms, landing pages, mobile experience, and parent inquiry flow.
  • Proof and trust assets - support collecting and using testimonials, parent verification, case-study style proof, and center differentiators.
  • Reporting - visibility into ad spend, lead quality, booked tours, conversion rate, and enrollment progress.
