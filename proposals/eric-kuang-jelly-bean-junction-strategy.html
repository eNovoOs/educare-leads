Because Eric has been burned by low-priority leads before, this strategy prioritizes lead quality, verification, and conversion tracking over raw lead volume.

2. Positioning Strategy

Core message: large center resources, personal classroom feel

A well-resourced early learning center that still feels personal, well-staffed, and family-centered.

  • Programs from infants at 6 weeks through 6 years old
  • Before- and after-school programs
  • Successful summer camp with 145 children
  • Well-staffed classrooms and quality furniture
  • A leadership philosophy built around happiness for families, staff, and children

Eric's “run it like Costco” idea should come through as trust, consistency, value, care, and a happy experience for everyone involved.

3. Phase 1 - Tracking & Lead Quality Foundation

Timeline: Week 1-2

  • Define what counts as a qualified lead.
  • Create CRM stages: new lead, qualified, contacted, tour booked, tour showed, enrolled, lost / not fit.
  • Track source by channel: word of mouth, Google Ads, Meta ads, referral, website, phone, walk-in.
  • Add classroom, age-group, start timeline, and program-interest tags.
  • Review current Google Ads structure, search terms, landing page, and lead quality.
  • Create simple weekly reporting around leads, tours, enrollments, and conversion rates.

4. Phase 2 - Trust Assets & Proof

Timeline: Week 1-4

Eric said client verification, testimonials, case studies, and a clear gameplan would make him more confident. Those same assets will also make parents more confident.

  • Collect 10-15 written parent testimonials.
  • Collect 2-3 short video testimonials if possible.
  • Create a “why families choose Jelly Bean Junction” proof section.
  • Highlight the successful summer camp with 145 children.
  • Capture photos or short clips of classrooms, furniture, staff warmth, and center quality.
  • Build proof into ad creative, landing pages, follow-up texts, and tour reminders.
  • Prepare a monthly performance case-study format showing leads, tours, enrollments, and lessons learned.

5. Phase 3 - Campaign Launch

Timeline: Weeks 2-6

  • Test infant care starting at 6 weeks.
  • Test toddler and preschool early learning angles.
  • Test before- and after-school care offers.
  • Use “large center resources, smaller classroom feel” as a differentiator.
  • Filter leads by child age, desired start date, care schedule, program interest, contact info, and neighborhood.
  • Use instant SMS, email proof, call tasks, tour booking prompts, reminders, and post-tour follow-up.

6. Phase 4 - Conversion Optimization

Timeline: Month 2-4

  • Review lead-to-tour conversion rate.
  • Review tour show rate.
  • Review tour-to-enrollment conversion rate.
  • Identify where families drop off.
  • Improve call scripts, SMS scripts, and email follow-up.
  • Test testimonials and proof assets in ads and follow-up.

7. Phase 5 - Scale Toward 150

Timeline: Month 4-6

  • Prioritize rooms with the most immediate enrollment capacity.
  • Increase ad spend only when lead quality and conversion are clear.
  • Run retargeting to parents who clicked, visited, or started forms but did not book.
  • Promote limited openings by age group.
  • Add referral pushes to current families.
  • Use summer camp proof to support year-round enrollment.
  • Continue weekly pipeline review.
