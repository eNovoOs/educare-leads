# EduCare Leads — VSL Script

> **Placement:** `/apply` page video slot ("How we fill programs in 90 days")
> **Audience:** Owner/director of a childcare or education program (daycare, private school, or camp) doing ≥ $25k/month with open spots to fill.
> **Goal of the video:** Get them to complete the application and book the free strategy call.
> **Spoken length:** ~3:30–4:00 min · **Tone:** credible, private-school caliber — confident, not hypey.

Each beat below has **[VO]** (voiceover / what's said) and **[ON-SCREEN]** (text + visual direction for the Remotion build). Timecodes are approximate.

---

## 0:00 — Hook (pattern interrupt)
**[VO]** If you run a daycare, school, or camp and you've got open spots right now — empty spots are costing you between one and two thousand dollars a month. *Each.* And the worst part? It's quiet. There's no bill, no alarm. Just revenue you never see.

**[ON-SCREEN]**
- Big number ticking up: an empty-room counter `–$1,500/mo · –$18,000/yr`
- Headline: **"Every empty spot is silent revenue loss."**

---

## 0:25 — Problem agitation (the pain they feel)
**[VO]** And you already know the old playbook doesn't fix it. You rely on referrals and a little Google luck. You boost a post, you get a handful of tire-kickers, and the few good leads that do come in slip through the cracks because nobody followed up fast enough. Meanwhile you're the one running the program *and* trying to run the marketing.

**[ON-SCREEN]** — list crosses out, one line at a time:
- ~~Referrals & "Google luck"~~
- ~~Boosted posts → tire-kickers~~
- ~~Leads that never get a callback~~
- ~~You doing marketing *and* running the program~~

---

## 0:55 — Reframe (it's not a marketing problem)
**[VO]** Here's the truth: you don't have a marketing problem. You have a *system* problem. Enrolling families isn't luck — it's a system. Most programs just never built one.

**[ON-SCREEN]** Big text: **"It's not a marketing problem. It's a system problem."**

---

## 1:10 — Introduce the mechanism (the 3-step system)
**[VO]** So we built it for you. It's called the Educare Enrollment System, and it runs in three steps.

**Step one — we build your system.** Hyper-local Meta and Google campaigns, written, designed, and targeted to families within driving distance of your front door. You don't write a single ad.

**Step two — AI converts every lead.** The second a family raises their hand, our AI texts them, qualifies them, and books them — onto a call, a tour, a visit, a registration. No chasing. No missed messages. No new hire.

**Step three — you enroll, and we scale.** Families show up ready to enroll. We track every lead in your CRM, double down on what's working, and pour more fuel in as your waitlist grows.

**[ON-SCREEN]** Three numbered cards animate in:
- **01 · We Build Your Enrollment System** — hyper-local Meta & Google ads
- **02 · AI Converts Every Lead** — instant text · qualify · book
- **03 · You Enroll & We Scale** — CRM tracking · scale to a waitlist

---

## 1:55 — Proof (numbers)
**[VO]** This isn't theory. We've done it for thirty-seven-plus programs across the country — daycares, private schools, and camps. Booked families for an average of fourteen dollars and ten cents. An average four-point-two times return on every dollar of ad spend. And a full calendar in ninety days.

**[ON-SCREEN]** Stat bar counts up:
- **$14.10** avg. cost per booked family
- **4.2×** average return on ad spend
- **37+** programs filled across the U.S.
- **90 days** to a full calendar

James, an admissions director at a private school in Florida, packed his open-house calendar for the first time in years. Aisha sold out her summer camp three weeks early and started a waitlist.

## 2:55 — The Offer + risk reversal (the close)
**[VO]** Here's the offer. We build the campaigns, we install the AI follow-up, we set up the CRM, and we hand you a ninety-day enrollment playbook. And we back the whole thing with the strongest guarantee in this industry: we'll get your *first qualified families booked within seven days* — or we work for free until they show up.

**[ON-SCREEN]** Value stack assembles, then the guarantee stamps on top:
- Educare Enrollment System campaigns — *built for you*
- AI lead-conversion system — *instant follow-up*
- CRM setup + weekly optimization
- 90-day enrollment playbook
- → Stamp: **"First families in 7 days — or we work free until they arrive."**

**[VO]** One catch: we only work with one program per area — we will not run ads for your competitor down the street — and we take a limited number of new programs each month. If your program does at least twenty-five thousand a month and you've got spots to fill, book your free strategy call right now. We'll map out your exact numbers — projected leads, cost per enrollment, and a ninety-day plan for your market. No cost, no obligation. Just fill out the short application on this page and pick a time.

**[ON-SCREEN]**
- **"One program per area. Limited spots each month."**
- Big CTA: **Book Your Free Strategy Call →**
- Sub: *Fill out the application on this page · takes 2 minutes*
