# Case Study Evidence Audit

Internal source notes for the public case study at `/case-studies`.

## Client Scope

- Anonymized multi-location Canadian summer camp.
- Campaign naming in the source accounts shows English, French, all-location, Toronto, Ottawa, Search, Performance Max, lead-form, and remarketing activity.
- No testimonial, booked-tour count, registration count, enrollment revenue, or ROAS is published without a CRM, calendar, payment, or client-confirmed source.

## Meta Ads

Source: Meta Ads Manager, Laurus Ad Account. Reporting period displayed as January 1 to July 30, 2026.

Meta displayed dollar values with a `$` symbol and no currency prefix in the audited view. The public page preserves that display instead of assigning a currency code.

| Public label | Source campaign | Leads | Cost / lead | Spend | Impressions |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| General lead campaign | LSC \| Leads \| $20 | 175 | $4.04 | $707.85 | 49,454 |
| Partner relaunch campaign | LSC \| Leads \| YMCA RELAUNCH | 339 | $2.63 | $892.02 | 70,432 |
| French-language campaign | Language Camp FRE \| Lead Form \| Per Location | 557 | $7.26 | $4,043.82 | 282,684 |
| Remarketing campaign | LSC \| Leads \| Remarketing | 77 | $3.58 | $275.71 | 15,905 |
| Location lead campaign | LSC \| Leads \| Ottawa | 81 | $6.13 | $496.47 | 29,344 |
| **Total / blended** | Five campaigns above | **1,229** | **$5.22** | **$6,415.87** | **447,819** |

Blended cost per lead calculation: `$6,415.87 / 1,229 = $5.2204`, displayed as `$5.22`.

Meta identifies the results as `Leads (Form)`. They are not represented as booked appointments or registrations.

## Combined Figure

`447,819 Meta impressions + 423,980 Google impressions = 871,799 reported ad impressions`.

This figure is channel delivery, not unique reach. The same person may have seen ads more than once or on both platforms.

## Missing Proof Layer

1. Qualified inquiries.
2. Calls or tours booked.
3. Calls or tours attended.
4. Registrations or enrollments.
5. Collected revenue attributable to those registrations.
6. Client approval for any testimonial, logo, or identifiable screenshot.
