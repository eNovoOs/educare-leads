## 2. ROLE

Contractor will assist EduCare Leads with business development and sales by identifying prospects, communicating with potential clients, and helping close deals for EduCare Leads (the "Services").

Contractor may not make promises, guarantees, discounts, or contractual commitments on behalf of EduCare Leads unless EduCare Leads approves them in writing.

## 3. COMMISSION

EduCare Leads will pay Contractor a commission equal to **ten percent (10%) of Net Cash Collected** by EduCare Leads from each Closed Deal that Contractor closes for EduCare Leads.

For purposes of this Agreement:

- A **"Closed Deal"** means a customer introduced, sold, or closed by Contractor that is accepted by EduCare Leads and results in EduCare Leads receiving payment from that customer.
- **"Net Cash Collected"** means amounts actually received and retained by EduCare Leads from the customer, excluding taxes, refunds, chargebacks, credits, payment processing fees, third-party advertising spend, and other pass-through costs.
- The commission applies only to Net Cash Collected during the **first twelve (12) months** after EduCare Leads receives the customer's first payment for that Closed Deal.

Unless EduCare Leads agrees otherwise in writing, no commission is owed on amounts collected after the first twelve (12) months, on customers not attributable to Contractor, or on deals not accepted by EduCare Leads.

## 4. PAYMENT TIMING

Commissions will be calculated monthly and paid within **fifteen (15) days** after the end of each month in which EduCare Leads receives the applicable Net Cash Collected.

If EduCare Leads later refunds, credits, reverses, or does not retain any customer payment, EduCare Leads may offset the related commission against future payments or request repayment from Contractor.

## 5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

Contractor is an independent contractor and is not an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of EduCare Leads. Contractor is responsible for all taxes, insurance, licenses, expenses, and legal obligations related to Contractor's work under this Agreement.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Contractor will keep confidential all non-public information received from EduCare Leads, including pricing, client information, leads, sales materials, processes, and business plans. Contractor may use such information only to perform the Services.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party with written notice.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time. Termination does not affect commissions earned on Closed Deals accepted by EduCare Leads before the termination date, provided those commissions are based on Net Cash Collected during the applicable first twelve (12) month commission period.

## 8. GENERAL

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding Contractor's Services and commissions and supersedes all prior discussions or understandings on that subject. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Electronic signatures and copies of signatures will be treated as originals.
