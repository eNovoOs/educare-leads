EduCare Leads will provide the Client with access to and ongoing management of the **EduCare Leads ecosystem**, which includes:

- **Automations** — automated lead follow-up, messaging, and booking workflows.

**4.2 Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other Party. Fees already paid for the current billing period are non-refundable.

**4.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, EduCare Leads will cease the Services and may deactivate access to the EduCare Leads ecosystem. The Client retains ownership of its own business data, branding, and any Meta advertising account it funds directly.
